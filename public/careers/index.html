01 — SOFTWARE

Software Developer

Open

Own features end to end across our two products: the React front end, the Node/Express API, the MongoDB data model and the integrations in between. You will ship to production and talk to the people using it.

For FreightCompareMERN plus real logistics, freight or supply-chain experience is our top priority.
For ForusBizMERN plus ERP, accounting, inventory or GST/invoicing experience.

You will

  • Build product features from API to UI
  • Integrate carriers, payments, maps and messaging
  • Work on our LLM copilots and voice input
  • Keep production fast and reliable

We look for

  • Solid React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB
  • Relevant experience, or projects that prove it
  • Code you can show us (GitHub, live apps)
  • Clear thinking and clear writing

Nice to have

  • Logistics, freight or ERP domain knowledge
  • TypeScript, Redis, cloud deploys
  • Android / Capacitor or React Native
  • LLM integrations
Branches: CSE, IT, EEE · with relevant experience Apply for this role
02 — HARDWARE

Hardware Innovator

Open

We want exceptionally good makers: people who design a board, write its firmware, solder it, power it up, find out why it does not work and fix it. Titles matter less than what is on your bench.

You will

  • Take ideas from schematic to working prototype
  • Design PCBs and write embedded firmware
  • Work on power electronics and battery systems
  • Test, measure, iterate, document

We look for

  • Things you have actually built, with photos or repos
  • PCB design (KiCad, Altium or similar)
  • Embedded C on STM32, ESP32 or similar
  • Comfort with a scope, a multimeter and an iron

Nice to have

  • BMS, motor control or SMPS work
  • Mechanical CAD and 3D printing
  • Competition teams: SAE, Robocon, e-Yantra
  • Anything that shipped to real users
Branches: EEE, ECE · freshers with strong builds welcome Apply for this role
